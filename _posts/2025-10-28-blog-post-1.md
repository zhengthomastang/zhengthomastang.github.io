October brought together our industrial vision AI work and the research community around multi-camera 3D perception. Our Foxconn factory digital-twin demonstration appeared in NVIDIA's GTC DC 2025 keynote, and I hosted the 9th AI City Challenge workshop at ICCV 2025 in Honolulu.

## Highlights

- **Factory digital twins:** The demonstration combined Metropolis, Cosmos, and Omniverse to analyze activity in a Foxconn factory environment and illustrate spatial monitoring workflows.
- **AI City Challenge:** The workshop brought together 245 teams from 15 countries, with work spanning multi-camera 3D perception, transportation safety, warehouse analytics, and edge-optimized fisheye detection.
- **Benchmark adoption:** PAISS and WTS Traffic Safety had nearly 400,000 combined Hugging Face downloads at this workshop checkpoint.
- **BMTT keynote:** I presented MCBLT/BEV-SUSHI, Sparse4D, and graph-based association for scalable multi-camera tracking at the 8th Workshop on Benchmarking Multi-Target Tracking.

Thank you to the teams across NVIDIA, Yizhou Wang, Sameer Pusegaonkar, and collaborators in Laura Leal-Taixé's group.
