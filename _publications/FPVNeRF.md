## Summary

FPV-NeRF addresses novel-view synthesis from UAV video, where limited viewpoints and changing scene scales challenge conventional radiance fields. The method combines temporal consistency, global structural cues, and multi-resolution supervision to preserve scene detail. The work also introduces a UAV dataset spanning indoor and outdoor trajectories, enabling evaluation of first-person-view generation and spatial representations for aerial scenes.
