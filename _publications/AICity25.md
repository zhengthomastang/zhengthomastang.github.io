## Summary

The ninth AI City Challenge evaluated multi-class 3D multi-camera tracking, traffic-safety video question answering, spatial reasoning in warehouse environments, and efficient fisheye road-object detection. It registered 245 teams from 15 countries on the evaluation server. Omniverse-generated datasets supported the tracking and spatial-reasoning tracks. The paper describes the datasets, held-out evaluation controls, and results across the four tasks.
