## Summary

The fourth AI City Challenge brought together 315 teams from 37 countries to study vehicle counting, vehicle re-identification, multi-camera vehicle tracking, and traffic anomaly detection. Participants used real traffic video and synthetic training data. Separate public and general leaderboards distinguished results produced under the contest's data restrictions from broader submissions. The paper summarizes the datasets, evaluation criteria, and challenge outcomes.
