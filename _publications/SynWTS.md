## Summary

SynWTS pairs synthetic digital-twin videos with real recordings from the Woven Traffic Safety dataset. It contains 249 paired scenarios and 846 synchronized videos from overhead and ego-vehicle perspectives, with bounding boxes, textual descriptions, visual question-answer pairs, and accident phases. This pairing supports direct study of synthetic-to-real transfer and the effect of visual realism on traffic-safety reasoning.
