## Summary

This method extracts melodies from polyphonic Western opera recordings using the singer's formant, a spectral feature near 3 kHz. It combines formant detection, the Fan Chirp Transform, and subharmonic attenuation to handle vibrato and octave errors. Experiments compare the approach with five melody-extraction methods on a newly collected dataset and ADC2004 recordings, evaluating formant detection, voicing, and overall melody accuracy.
