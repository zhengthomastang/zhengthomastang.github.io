## Abstract
The 10th AI City Challenge, held with ECCV 2026, marks a decade of community benchmarking for intelligent transportation, smart cities, and physical AI. The 2026 edition attracted 325 registered teams from 26 countries and regions and featured six primary tracks spanning multi-camera 3D perception, transportation safety captioning and VQA, traffic anomaly reasoning, text-based person anomaly search, generative traffic video forecasting, and cross-city object detection. The challenge evaluated systems across synthetic-to-real learning, multimodal reasoning, retrieval and reranking, geometric grounding, generative forecasting, and privacy-preserving evaluation.
