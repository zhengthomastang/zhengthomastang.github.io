## Summary

Traffic Anomaly Reasoning is a multi-task dataset for vision-language model training and evaluation. It contains 44,040 training annotations across ten task types and 3,670 transportation videos, covering question answering, temporal reasoning, and scene understanding. Human-reviewed evaluation highlights remaining difficulties in deeper reasoning, while multi-task fine-tuning improves performance. The dataset supports AI City Challenge 2026 Track 3 and is released under CC BY 4.0.
