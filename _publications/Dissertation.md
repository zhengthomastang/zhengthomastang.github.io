## Summary

This dissertation connects camera self-calibration, adaptive object tracking, and human pose estimation for 3D scene reconstruction. It develops methods for estimating camera parameters from walking people, learning target appearance online, and jointly recovering human pose and camera motion. The work also combines visual, geometric, and semantic cues across camera views to reconstruct trajectories and 3D human skeletons.
