## Summary

This framework tracks people within and across non-overlapping cameras without supervised camera-link training. It combines adaptive segmentation and tracking, a two-stage feature extractor, and an online model of relationships between cameras. Appearance, contextual information, and automatically detected companion pairs support cross-camera association. Experiments on NLPR_MCT evaluate the method's ability to scale across a camera network.
