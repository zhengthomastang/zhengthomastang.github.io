## Summary

This framework studies knowledge distillation for video classification under limited supervision. It combines substage-based teacher-student alignment, progressive cascade training, pseudo-label refinement, and a feature-distribution loss. These components address differences between teacher and student capacity while transferring complementary information across network stages. Experiments on real and simulated video datasets evaluate the resulting classification performance.
