## Summary

The third AI City Challenge evaluated city-scale vehicle tracking, vehicle re-identification, and traffic anomaly detection using real traffic-camera data. It attracted 334 academic and industry teams from 44 countries, with 129 submissions across the three tracks. The paper describes the datasets, evaluation protocols, and participating methods, examining how video analysis can support transportation and traffic operations.
