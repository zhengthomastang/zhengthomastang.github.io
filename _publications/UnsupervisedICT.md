## Summary

This method associates people across non-overlapping cameras through fully unsupervised online learning. A two-stage feature extractor combines appearance and contextual information, including automatically detected companion pairs, while a camera-link model learns relationships between views. Experiments on NLPR_MCT evaluate cross-camera tracking performance and the scalability of the approach without manually labeled camera-link data.
