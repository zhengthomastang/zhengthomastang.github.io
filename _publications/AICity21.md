## Summary

The fifth AI City Challenge evaluated vehicle counting, re-identification, multi-camera tracking, anomaly detection, and vehicle retrieval from natural-language descriptions. It attracted 305 teams from 38 countries and combined real traffic data with synthetic training data. Public and general leaderboards separated contest-compliant methods from unrestricted submissions. The paper reports the task definitions, evaluation protocols, and results across all five tracks.
