## Summary

This method estimates the ground plane for 3D object tracking from a moving monocular camera. Structure from motion provides camera pose estimates, while camera yaw feeds back into ground-plane refinement. The approach combines this geometry with constrained multiple-kernel tracking to handle changing road conditions, calibration noise, and occlusion. Experiments on challenging video datasets evaluate the resulting tracking performance.
