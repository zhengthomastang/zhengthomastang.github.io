## Summary

This work studies synthetic training data for camera-based automated retail checkout. DtTrain adjusts the viewpoint and illumination of product digital twins to match representative real checkout images, preserving product labels while reflecting user handling patterns. The resulting data improve product counting compared with other synthesis methods. Combining the digital-twin dataset with pseudo-labeled real checkout video provides further improvement.
