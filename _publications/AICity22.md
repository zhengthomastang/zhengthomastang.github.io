## Summary

The sixth AI City Challenge covered transportation and retail through four tracks: multi-camera vehicle tracking, natural-language vehicle retrieval, driver-action recognition, and single-camera automated checkout. It received participation requests from 254 teams across 27 countries. The paper presents the datasets, task definitions, and evaluation protocols, with separate leaderboards for contest-compliant and general submissions.
