## Summary

The seventh AI City Challenge introduced multi-camera people tracking and motorcycle helmet-rule violation detection alongside vehicle retrieval, driver-action recognition, and automated checkout. It received participation requests from 508 teams across 46 countries. Real and synthetic data supported the five tracks, while separate leaderboards distinguished contest-compliant results. The paper summarizes the datasets, protocols, participating approaches, and benchmark outcomes.
