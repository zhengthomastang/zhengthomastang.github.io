## Summary

PhysicalAI-SmartSpaces is a benchmark for multi-class, multi-camera 3D perception in indoor environments. It provides more than 280 hours of synchronized video from nearly 1,800 cameras, with identities, camera calibration, and 2D and 3D annotations. The benchmark combines Isaac Sim generation, Cosmos appearance augmentation, and real-world evaluation. Standardized protocols and 3D Higher Order Tracking Accuracy support evaluation of localization and tracking.
