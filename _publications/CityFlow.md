## Summary

CityFlow is a city-scale benchmark for multi-camera vehicle tracking and re-identification. It contains more than three hours of synchronized video from 40 cameras across ten intersections, with over 200,000 annotated bounding boxes and camera-calibration information. The paper evaluates detection, tracking, and re-identification baselines and examines the effects of model architecture, loss functions, and spatial-temporal information on performance.
