## Summary

WSSGCN studies a wider, staged alternative to deep graph convolutional networks. Its architecture combines node and edge, subgraph, and global features, with response-, feature-, and relationship-based consistency across stages. A width-expansion strategy supports efficient training. Experiments on graph benchmarks compare accuracy and training speed with conventional GCN architectures.
