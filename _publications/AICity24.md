## Summary

The eighth AI City Challenge attracted 726 teams from 47 countries and regions across five tracks: multi-camera people tracking, traffic-safety video captioning, driver-action recognition, fisheye-camera analytics, and motorcycle helmet-rule violation detection. Its tracking benchmark expanded camera coverage and 3D annotations using synthetic environments. The paper describes each dataset and evaluation protocol and reviews the results of participating systems.
